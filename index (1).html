I’m a Mechanical Engineering major with a Mechatronics focus at the FAMU/FSU College of Engineering, with hands-on experience across aerospace, refrigeration and packaging manufacturing. Across four internships and co-op rotations — including two with Delta Air Lines — I’ve worked on aircraft assemblies, industrial robotics, production fixtures and embedded hardware.

What drives me is the intersection of mechanical design and electronics. Whether it’s building an autonomous quadcopter from scratch, designing a rotor current meter for divers or improving a robot cell to cut scrap, I’m happiest turning an idea into something physical and functional.
